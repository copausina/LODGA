HB 652- by Rep Jacob Braud. adds a member to the Task Force from Dept of Agriculture . Result  Act 81

HB 653 by Rep Mike Bayham. adds that one of the two members to the Task Force from the United Commercial Fisherman's Association be a resident from St Bernard Parish. Result Act 82

HB 669 by Rep Joseph Orgeron. Allows the Grand Isle Port Commission to utilize 27.5 acres for Off Bottom research by  the Louisiana Sea Grant program.  Result sent to governor

HB 121 by Rep Jessica Domangue. Expands authority of Dept of Agriculture to regulate and enforce laws governing imported seafood. Result sent to Governor

HB 889 by Rep Joseph Orgeron. Establishes a bulk tagging process for oysters harvested under alternative cultivation methods. Result passed senate and enrolled.

HB 770  would change the term of an oyster lease from 15 years to 5 years. Result- the bill was deferred.
